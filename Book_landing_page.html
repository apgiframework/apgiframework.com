About the Author

Dimitry Pesochin is a consciousness researcher specializing in predictive processing and computational models of awareness.

With over a decade of research at the intersection of Bayesian brain theory and phenomenology, Pesochin developed the APGI Framework to answer a personal question: Why did his meditation practice feel so inconsistent? This curiosity led to a comprehensive investigation into the architecture of conscious experience.

Pesochin's work synthesizes neuroscience, philosophy, and contemplative practice to create actionable models of awareness. His research has been featured in consciousness studies conferences and he maintains an active dialogue with both academic researchers and contemplative practitioners.

When not researching consciousness, Pesochin practices meditation (which still has foggy days—now he knows why) and explores the practical applications of APGI in clinical and educational settings.
