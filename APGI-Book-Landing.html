Inside the Book

32 chapters. One unified argument.

Structured in four parts — from the neuroscience of gating to practical applications in anxiety, flow, creativity, and decision-making. Every chapter ends with a parameter exercise.

Part I · Chapters 1–8
The Precision Problem
Why the brain can't trust its own signals — and how interoceptive precision weighting determines which body states reach conscious awareness. Includes heartbeat detection data from 6 studies.
Part I · Chapters 5–10
Prediction Error as Fuel
The surprise signal that drives ignition. Why unexpected events feel more vivid, why novelty is addictive, and why routine kills conscious depth. Based on Rao & Ballard (1999) and Friston (2010).
Part II · Chapters 11–16
The Thalamic Gate
How Bt is set, what raises or lowers it, and why it varies across individuals, states, and pharmacological conditions. The architecture of the threshold you never knew you had.
Part II · Chapters 17–20
Ignition & Broadcast
When Π × |ε| exceeds Bt, the frontoparietal network floods with signal. This is the moment of conscious access — what Dehaene calls the global neuronal workspace igniting.
Part III · Chapters 21–27
Configuration Types
Anxiety, flow, depression, creativity, hyperfocus — each is a specific APGI parameter configuration. Not a disorder; a system setting. Includes 7 signature profiles with intervention protocols.
Part IV · Chapters 28–32
Calibrating Your System
Practical protocols for adjusting each parameter: threshold desensitization, precision training, somatic recalibration. Backed by Barrett & Simmons (2015) and clinical pilot data.
