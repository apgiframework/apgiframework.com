About APGI

A theoretical framework. A testable research programme.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

What APGI explores

Allostatic Precision-Gated Ignition (APGI) is a proposed computational model of consciousness ignition, precision-gated attention and allostatic regulation. It explores how prediction, attention and bodily regulation might contribute to what reaches conscious access.

The framework is a research proposal. Its mechanisms and measurement claims require empirical testing; publication of a manuscript or protocol does not establish validation.

Read the theory. Examine the tests.

Start with the research papers, then examine the empirical protocols and falsification criteria. These resources describe the proposed model and the conditions under which its claims should be revised or rejected.

For software integration, use the canonical API documentation. Research protocols describe experiments; API documentation describes the service contract.

Author and public record

The research is authored by Dimitry Pesochin. Public identifiers and materials are available through ORCID, the OSF project, the research registration and the archived v1.0 release. Source repositories are available on GitHub.

A research registration is a record of a study plan. It does not establish ethics approval for every pilot or validate an assessment.

Scope and questions

This site presents research and educational material. It does not provide a clinical diagnosis or treatment recommendation. Assessment outputs should not be used to make medical decisions.
