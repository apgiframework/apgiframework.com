Frequently asked questions

Last updated: 30 October 2026

What does APGI stand for?

Allostatic Precision-Gated Ignition. It is a proposed computational model of consciousness ignition, precision-gated attention and allostatic regulation.

Is APGI an established theory?

No. APGI is a research proposal. Its mechanisms and measurement claims require empirical testing, and publishing a manuscript or protocol does not establish validity. The falsification page lists the tests that could count against it.

Can the assessment diagnose a condition?

No. This site presents research and educational material. It does not provide clinical diagnosis or treatment recommendations, and assessment outputs should not be used to make medical decisions.

Where can I read the research?

Start with the research papers, then see the empirical protocol reference.

Where is the API documentation?

The canonical documentation is at api.apgiframework.com/docs. The protocol reference on this site describes experiments, not API behaviour.

How do I get the book?

The book page lists the direct EPUB download and audiobook via Gumroad. Retail-store listings are marked coming soon. See the About page for the author.

How do I report an error or ask a question?

Use the contact page for research questions, corrections or collaboration enquiries. Accessibility problems can be reported through the route on the accessibility statement.
