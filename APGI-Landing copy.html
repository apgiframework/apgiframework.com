The Three Parameters

Every conscious moment involves
the same three variables.

The APGI equation is not a metaphor. Each symbol encodes a measurable neural process — and each process has a corresponding behavioral signature you can assess.

Bt
Threshold Sensitivity
The dynamic thalamic gate that incoming signals must exceed to reach conscious broadcast. Lower thresholds mean easier ignition — heightened sensitivity and reactivity. Higher thresholds mean selective, stable awareness.
Neural basis: Thalamocortical gating, reticular activating system.
Dehaene & Changeux, 2011; Crick & Koch, 2003.
Π
Interoceptive Accuracy
The brain's confidence in its own body signals — precision weighting from the insular cortex and ACC. High Π amplifies every signal that crosses the threshold. It determines how much body-state information enters conscious workspace.
Neural basis: Insula, anterior cingulate cortex, vagal afferents.
Barrett & Simmons, 2015; Seth, 2013.
|ε|
Somatic Bias
The magnitude of prediction error — the mismatch between what the brain expects and what it receives. Large errors demand explanation and drive ignition. This is the surprise signal that makes unexpected events feel vivid and significant.
Neural basis: Superficial cortical layers, dopaminergic prediction error.
Friston, 2010; Rao & Ballard, 1999.
Ignition Sequence — 4 Stages
|ε|
Prediction error sparks
Something doesn't match the model. Superficial cortical layers fire a mismatch signal — the brain notices a discrepancy between prediction and input.
Π
Precision weighting applied
The insula and ACC evaluate the signal's reliability. High precision amplifies the error; low precision suppresses it. This is the body-reading step.
Bt
Thalamic gate evaluated
If Π × |ε| exceeds the current threshold, the thalamic gate opens. The system determines whether this signal deserves global broadcast.
GW
Global workspace ignites
Frontoparietal networks broadcast the signal widely. This is conscious access — the moment awareness crystallizes around the event.
How It Works

Consciousness is not continuous. It fires.

The global workspace does not stream — it ignites in discrete events. Each moment of conscious awareness is a threshold-crossing: a brief window where the right signal, weighted by the right precision, exceeds the gate and floods the frontoparietal network.

01
This isn't a bug in your experience
Anxiety is a low threshold with high precision weighting. Flow is a calibrated system running efficiently. Depression often involves elevated threshold with reduced error sensitivity. Each state is a configuration, not a failure.
02
The equation is falsifiable
APGI makes quantitatively testable predictions about neural dynamics, behavioral signatures, and pharmacological interventions. Each parameter has a corresponding intervention target.
03
Your configuration is measurable
Through behavioral proxy measures — interoceptive accuracy tasks, threshold detection protocols, and error sensitivity assessments — your personal parameter space can be estimated.
