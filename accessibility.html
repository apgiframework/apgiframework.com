Accessibility Statement

Research should be readable and reachable.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Our approach

We aim to make the APGI Framework website usable with a keyboard, screen reader, browser zoom and different screen sizes. WCAG 2.2 Level AA is our target for ongoing improvements. We have not established sitewide conformance.

These information pages include a skip link, semantic navigation and main landmarks, ordered headings, visible keyboard focus and layouts that adapt to narrow screens. Their content can be read without JavaScript.

Known limitations

Accessibility work is still in progress across the existing site. Earlier checks identified contrast, heading-order and landmark issues on the papers index. Navigation patterns also vary between pages.

Interactive assessments, charts and downloadable documents need further review for keyboard use, screen-reader descriptions and accessible alternatives. External services, including the API documentation interface, require their own accessibility review.

Historical automated scores do not establish current accessibility or replace testing with assistive technology.

Reading and navigation

You can use your browser’s zoom and text controls. On these information pages, press Tab to reveal “Skip to content” and move between links; press Enter to follow a link. No animation or timed interaction is needed to read these pages.

If a diagram or interactive view prevents access to a research explanation, report the page and the information you need so an alternative can be considered.

Report an accessibility barrier

Use the email details on the contact page to report a barrier. Include the page address, what you were trying to do, what happened and, if you are comfortable sharing them, your browser and assistive technology. Please avoid sending sensitive health or assessment data.

Reports will help prioritise fixes. This statement will be updated as barriers are reviewed and addressed.
