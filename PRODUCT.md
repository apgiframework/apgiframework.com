# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Customers booking appointments with service providers. They need to quickly view available times, select a slot, provide details, and pay—all with minimal friction and maximum clarity.

## Product Purpose

A fast, beautiful appointment booking system that prioritizes simplicity and speed. Users can browse available times, book, and pay in three intuitive steps. Service providers get a lightweight way to accept appointments and payments via Stripe, without complex scheduling management.

## Positioning

The fastest way to book an appointment. No unnecessary steps, no friction, just availability → selection → payment.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, trustworthy, delightful. The app moves quickly but never feels rushed. Clean, intentional, respectful of the user's time.

## Anti-references

- Overcomplicated scheduling flows with unnecessary questions
- Cluttered calendars with visual noise
- Unclear pricing or hidden fees during checkout
- Outdated, corporate design language

## Design Principles

1. **Speed first**: every interaction removes friction, not adds it
2. **Visual clarity**: information hierarchy guides users naturally through the workflow
3. **Trust through design**: clean, intentional details build confidence
4. **Purposeful simplicity**: every element earns its place; nothing is decorative

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA standard. Full keyboard navigation, color-blind safe palettes, sufficient contrast (≥4.5:1 for body text), and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
