What is APGI?

Allostatic Precision-Gated Ignition is a model of consciousness that treats awareness not as a mysterious property of the brain, but as a threshold event — like a circuit breaker flipping. Your nervous system is always predicting. When something unexpected happens and the body is confident about that surprise, the signal crosses a gate and floods the brain's global workspace. That flood is conscious experience.

Π

Body Confidence

How strongly your nervous system trusts the surprise signal it just detected. This is shaped by arousal, stress hormones, and your internal physiological state at that moment — what researchers call "allostatic precision."

In plain terms: how alert and tuned-in your body is right now
|ε|

Surprise Magnitude

The size of the gap between what your brain predicted would happen and what actually happened. A small discrepancy passes unnoticed. A large one demands attention — this is the raw signal that might become conscious.

In plain terms: how unexpected was that event?
Bt

Awareness Threshold

The gating barrier set by your thalamus — a deep brain structure that acts as a filter. Only signals whose confidence-weighted surprise exceeds this threshold break through to conscious broadcast. Sleep, drugs, and attention all shift it.

In plain terms: how high is the bar for something to reach awareness?

"Consciousness is not a lamp that's always on or always off. It's a gate that opens when the right signal arrives at the right moment — and APGI describes precisely what 'right' means."

— Framework summary, Paper I (Pesochin, 2024)
🧬

Grounded in established science

APGI synthesizes three well-supported frameworks — predictive processing (Friston), global workspace theory (Dehaene), and allostatic regulation (Sterling, Barrett) — into a single coherent model.

📐

A testable prediction

APGI generates concrete, measurable predictions about when awareness should occur, built on established findings from predictive processing, global workspace theory, and allostatic regulation research. Empirical validation of APGI's own predictions is ongoing.

The Research Series

Four papers trace APGI from first principles to clinical implications, each building on the last. Supplemental materials provide raw data, experimental stimuli, and extended literature reviews for researchers who want to go deeper.

I

The APGI Framework: A Unified Account of Conscious Ignition

Introduces the core equation and its three components. Reviews the predictive processing and global workspace literatures that motivate the framework. Establishes how allostatic state modulates precision weighting and why this changes the threshold for conscious broadcast. Foundational paper — start here.

Theoretical Framework Literature Review
PDF → Supp →
II

Allostatic Modulation of Precision: Empirical Evidence and Model Validation

Tests whether allostatic state (indexed by interoceptive measures) predicts precision-weighted prediction error magnitudes in behavioral tasks. Presents correlation data across multiple paradigms. Validates the model's predictions against neuroimaging and psychophysical data from existing studies.

Empirical Validation Neuroimaging
PDF → Data → Supp →
III

Threshold Dynamics: Sleep, Arousal, and the Gating of Conscious Access

Examines how the threshold parameter Bt varies across states of consciousness — wakefulness, sleep stages, anesthesia, and altered states. Proposes a neurobiological substrate for the threshold gate and reviews the thalamo-cortical mechanisms that implement it. Includes clinical implications for disorders of consciousness.

Threshold Sleep Science Clinical
PDF → Supp →
IV

From Mechanism to Mind: Implications of APGI for Consciousness Studies, Psychiatry, and AI

The culminating paper. Addresses what APGI implies for longstanding debates — the hard problem, phenomenal vs. access consciousness, artificial consciousness. Proposes experimental designs that could falsify the framework. Discusses applications to psychiatric conditions where allostatic dysregulation disrupts conscious experience.

Philosophy of Mind Psychiatry AI Implications
PDF → Supp →
