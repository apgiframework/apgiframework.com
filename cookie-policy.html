Cookie Policy

Understand what your browser stores.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small values a website can ask your browser to store and send with later requests. Local storage is separate browser storage that a page can read on the same origin; it is not automatically sent with every request.

Some existing pages use local storage to remember a theme preference. That preference can remain until you clear site data. This page does not set cookies or use local storage.

Analytics and optional features

The website source includes an analytics module, but it is not currently enabled as a sitewide analytics integration. This policy does not announce activation of analytics, advertising or tracking.

Before optional tracking is enabled, this policy must identify the provider, purpose, stored values and retention period. Where consent is required, optional tracking must remain disabled until you choose to enable it, with a way to withdraw that choice.

External resources and services

Pages may load fonts or libraries from external providers. This page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests share connection information, such as your IP address, with the provider even when no cookie is set.

The API documentation and other external links open separate services. Their storage and privacy practices may differ from those of this static website. Read their notices when you use them.

Manage your choices

Use your browser’s privacy settings to inspect, block or delete cookies and clear site data for apgiframework.com. Clearing local storage can reset saved preferences. Browser settings may treat the API subdomain separately.

For more context, read the Privacy Policy. To ask about browser storage, use the details on the contact page. This notice will be updated when storage practices change.
