Hook → Authority → Payoff → CTA Formula
0–3 SECONDS · HOOK
Pattern Interrupt
White bold text. Short. Declarative. Fragmented for emphasis. The first 1 second must earn the next 14. No color until the claim lands.
Examples: "Your brain has a bug." · "Consciousness isn't what you think." · "Flow state? Just math."
4–8 SECONDS · AUTHORITY FLASH
Evidence + Mechanism
Introduce APGI parameters with semantic color. Blue for threshold, Red for precision, Yellow for equation. Citation pill at top-left. Equation badge optional.
Citation pill format: "Barrett 2017 · Predictive Processing"
9–12 SECONDS · COMPASSIONATE PAYOFF
Pain → Mechanism
Red left-border card. Acknowledge experience → name the APGI parameter → explain why. Validate without diagnosis. State language: "your system is in X configuration".
Template: "This is why [struggle] — your [parameter] is [state]. [Mechanism]."
13–15 SECONDS · CTA
Purple Button
Filled purple pill button. Always specific and action-oriented. "Full framework in bio" OR "Take the quiz (link in bio)". Never generic "follow me".
Approved: "Full framework in bio" · "Take the APGI quiz →" · "Understanding your signature →"
