The Paradox

Physically identical stimuli sometimes reach awareness and sometimes stay unconscious. Existing theories describe broadcasting or integration, but none predict the trial-to-trial boundary. Without a precise account of threshold dynamics, the field cannot say when a signal crosses into consciousness.

The ecosystem

APGI ships as an open scientific core. Higher layers — tooling, training, simulation environments, and application interfaces — sit on top of that core.

The goal isn't a single product. It's a shared reference framework that researchers can test, clinicians can translate, educators can teach, and builders can implement, while the foundational claims stay open, versioned, and accountable to evidence.
